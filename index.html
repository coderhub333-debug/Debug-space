Our story

Fewer dresses, made better.

ÉLARA began in a two-room atelier in Lisbon with a simple frustration: beautiful dresses were either disposable or unaffordable. We set out to make a third option — pieces cut in small runs, from mills we visit ourselves, at prices that reflect the cloth rather than the logo.

Every silhouette is fitted on real bodies across five sizes before it goes into production, and every seam is finished by hand. We release two collections a year, not twelve, because good design needs time to settle.
